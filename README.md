# Propnexaa Outbound Copilot — Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)

A high-performance personal Chrome Extension for **LinkedIn & Instagram Outreach, 1-Click Connection Notes, Thought-Leadership Commenting, and Viral Post Creation**.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome (Takes 30 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. In the URL address bar, type `chrome://extensions` and press **Enter**.
3. In the top-right corner, turn ON **"Developer mode"**.
4. In the top-left corner, click the **"Load unpacked"** button.
5. Select this folder:
   ```
   d:\Hostbolt\new me\07_Propnexaa_Outreach_Extension
   ```
6. **Done!** The extension icon `⚡ Propnexaa Outbound Copilot` will appear in your Chrome toolbar.
7. Click the **Pin** icon next to it so it stays visible on your Chrome bar.

---

## ⚡ How to Use

### 1. LinkedIn 1-Click Connection Requests (< 300 chars)
* Navigate to any broker's or founder's LinkedIn profile (e.g. `https://www.linkedin.com/in/...`).
* Click the `⚡ Propnexaa` icon in your toolbar — the **Side Panel** opens on the right!
* It automatically detects the broker's name, headline, and company.
* Click **"✨ Generate Tailored Message"** with *Connection Note* selected.
* It writes a bespoke note under 280 characters.
* Click **"⚡ Insert into Page"** or copy to paste directly when clicking "Add a note" on LinkedIn!

### 2. Lavender 40-Word DMs & InMails
* Select **"Lavender 40-Word DM"**.
* Generates a peer-to-peer cold DM with double line breaks, 5th-grade simple English, and 1 soft question.

### 3. Smart Feed Commenting (Engagement Hacking)
* Scroll your LinkedIn or Instagram feed.
* Click the **"✨ AI Comment"** button injected under any post.
* Choose your angle:
  * 💡 *Thoughtful Industry Insight*
  * ❓ *Curious Follow-Up Question*
  * ⚡ *Short & Punchy Endorsement*
* Click **"⚡ Insert into Comment Box"** to paste it directly!

### 4. Instagram Luxury Broker Outreach
* Open any Dubai luxury agent's Instagram profile or reel.
* Generates natural, non-spammy Story Replies / DM Icebreakers referencing their active listings and walkthroughs.

### 5. Viral Post Creator
* Under the **"Post Creator"** tab, enter a raw thought (e.g. *"Dubai after-hours lead drop"*).
* Select style (*Problem + Breakdown*, *Contrarian Hot-Take*, *Case Study*).
* Instantly drafts a viral post with scroll-stopping hooks and clean spacing.
